## 0.26.0

Baseline. The versioning scheme starts here, anchored at twenty-six built
milestones, M1 through M26. The last of these, "A Write the Disk Keeps Across
a Boot," made the user-space virtio-mmio store answer FS_OP_WRITE and keep the
write across a reboot. Every milestone's retrospective is in docs/ROADMAP.md.
